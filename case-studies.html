Google Maps Lead Finder

Problem: Finding business leads on Google Maps meant hours of copying names and phone numbers by hand.
What was built: A tool built with Python and Playwright (a robot web browser) that searches Google Maps, collects business details and removes duplicates automatically. Related service: data scraping & automation.
Result: Lead research that used to be manual copy-paste now runs on its own, and the list arrives clean and ready to call.
