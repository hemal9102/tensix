Common Questions

Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

What is industrial web engineering for manufacturers?

Industrial web engineering is the practice of developing high-throughput, latency-optimized digital infrastructure specifically for B2B manufacturers, engineering plants, and industrial exporters. Unlike generic marketing websites, industrial portals feature sub-400ms global response times, technical CAD/PDF spec sheets, multi-tier RFQ inquiry pipelines, and secure ERP integrations with systems like SAP and Tally.

Why do Ahmedabad machinery exporters lose global contracts on traditional agency websites?

Most traditional local agency sites are built on bloated WordPress installations that take 2,500ms to 4,500ms to load overseas in Germany, the US, or UAE. Overseas B2B procurement engineers bounce immediately when spec sheets take seconds to open. Furthermore, legacy agency sites lack structured schema graphs and pass shared RFQ leads to third-party broker marketplaces.

How does TENSIX achieve sub-400ms page load speeds globally?

TENSIX engineers zero-bloat static and edge-rendered architectures deployed across 300+ global edge nodes. By eliminating server-side PHP bottlenecks, stripping unused CSS frameworks, and compiling product catalogs into lean static HTML/JSON, our websites hit 404ms or faster worldwide, passing Core Web Vitals with 100/100 Lighthouse scores.

Can our RFQ portal connect directly into Tally, SAP, or our custom ERP?

Yes. Every RFQ submitted on your TENSIX industrial portal is structured with clean JSON payloads. We engineer secure webhook bridges and REST API connectors to route inquiries directly into your existing Tally, SAP, Zoho CRM, or custom database, accompanied by instant WhatsApp notifications to your sales directors.

Who owns the source code and infrastructure?

You retain 100% ownership of all source code, design assets, and cloud deployment pipelines from day one. Everything is deployed directly to your GitHub repository and cloud accounts (Cloudflare/Vercel/AWS). No vendor lock-in, no licensing penalties, and zero proprietary entrapment.
