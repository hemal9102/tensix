Can TENSIX build a SaaS product or custom software for my business? +

Yes. A first working version of a SaaS product (an MVP, the smallest version you can sell) usually takes 14 to 21 business days. It typically includes user logins, online payments through Razorpay or Stripe, an admin area, and screens that work well on phones.

How are GEO and AEO different from normal SEO? +

Normal SEO (search engine optimisation) helps your pages rank in Google results. GEO and AEO (generative and answer engine optimisation) help AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews understand your business and mention it in their answers. This is done by stating clear facts on your pages and adding structured data (labels that machines can read). Nobody can guarantee a top spot, but it gives you a much better chance of being found and quoted.

What can an AI agent do for my business? +

An AI agent is software that can read, decide and act on routine tasks for you. For example, it can answer customer questions from your own documents, sort incoming emails, or prepare simple reports. TENSIX builds these with tools like LangGraph and models from Anthropic and OpenAI, and sets them up to show where each answer came from so your team can check it.

Can you stop my business emails from going to spam? +

TENSIX sets up the records that prove your emails are really from you (SPF, DKIM and DMARC), connects a reliable sending service such as Amazon SES, and handles bounced emails properly. This meets the Gmail and Yahoo sender rules and greatly improves your chances of reaching the inbox. No one can promise inbox placement, because your content and sending habits also matter.

How do data collection and inbox automation work? +

Small programs collect publicly available information from websites, or read incoming emails, PDF invoices and spreadsheets. The useful details are pulled out, cleaned and saved into Google Sheets, a database or your CRM (customer records system), so nobody has to copy them by hand.

What cloud server and hosting setup do you provide? +

TENSIX sets up and secures servers on Oracle Cloud, AWS, DigitalOcean or Plesk. This includes a firewall, free SSL certificates that renew on their own, automatic backups, and one-step releases from GitHub (CI/CD). Monitoring and automatic restarts are also set up, so problems are caught early.

Do you work with clients outside India (USA, UK, UAE, Europe, Australia)? +

Yes. All work can be done online, with written updates in English and calls at times that suit your time zone. International clients pay a fixed price in USD by Stripe or bank transfer.

Why a fixed price instead of hourly billing? +

Hourly billing makes costs hard to predict. TENSIX agrees a fixed price and a clear list of what you will get before work starts, with projects ranging from ₹75,000 to ₹3,50,000+ ($1,000 to $4,500+) (or the USD equivalent). You know the cost and the delivery date upfront, with no hidden charges.

Who owns the code and the accounts? +

You do, from day one. The code goes into your own GitHub or GitLab account and runs on your own cloud or hosting account. You get all passwords and keys, so you are never locked in to TENSIX.

How long does a project take? +

Smaller jobs, such as a data collector, an inbox automation or a website redesign, usually take 5 to 10 business days. Bigger projects, such as a SaaS product, a custom business system or an AI agent setup, take about 2 to 5 weeks, delivered in milestones.

Can you rebuild my slow WordPress or old PHP website? +

Yes. Slow WordPress, Elementor, Wix or old PHP sites are rebuilt as lightweight, fast-loading websites. Your old page addresses are redirected to the new ones (301 redirects), so you keep the Google rankings you already have.

How do you make websites load fast? +

Pages are kept light: clean code, no heavy page builders, compressed images, few scripts, and hosting on a CDN (a network that serves your site from a location near each visitor). Every site is checked with Google PageSpeed before launch.
