Client Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding our engineering sprints, pricing model, and technical integration.

How does TENSIX price engineering projects?

Every engagement is structured as a fixed-price sprint based on defined technical deliverables. You receive a clear, transparent scope and milestone schedule upfront, with zero surprise hourly bills.

What is the typical turnaround time for an engagement?

Most focused systems — such as automated workflow pipelines, custom data scrapers, or portal MVPs — are completed and deployed within 7 to 14 business days. Larger full-stack platforms are delivered in structured 2 to 4-week milestones.

Can you integrate with our existing software and database stack?

Yes. We engineer systems that seamlessly integrate with standard REST/GraphQL APIs, webhooks, PostgreSQL, Supabase, AWS, Cloudflare, and custom legacy backends without disrupting ongoing operations.

Who owns the intellectual property and code?

You do. Upon project completion and final milestone approval, 100% of the source code, cloud infrastructure configurations, and databases are transferred directly to your organization with full documentation.
