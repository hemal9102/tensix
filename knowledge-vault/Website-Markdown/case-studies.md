### Google Maps Lead Finder

**Problem:** Finding business leads on Google Maps meant hours of copying names and phone numbers by hand.
**What was built:** A tool built with Python and Playwright (a robot web browser) that searches Google Maps, collects business details and removes duplicates automatically. Related service: [data scraping & automation](https://www.tensix.in/services/data-scraping-automation).
**Result:** Lead research that used to be manual copy-paste now runs on its own, and the list arrives clean and ready to call.

### AtoZ Gadgetz – E-Commerce Platform

**Problem:** An online gadget store needed to accept payments from buyers in different countries.
**What was built:** A store with PayPal, Payoneer and Stripe checkout, automatic product management and full delivered-cost (shipping and duties) calculation.
**Result:** Buyers can pay the way they prefer and see the full price before they order.

### JobRecruitment.in – AI Recruitment Platform

**Problem:** Hiring is slow on both sides: companies sort resumes by hand and candidates hunt for openings.
**What was built:** A two-sided hiring website. Companies get applicant tracking with AI that reads resumes and ranks candidates. Job seekers get job search and instant alerts.
**Result:** Companies see the best-matched candidates first, and candidates hear about new jobs as soon as they are posted.

Node.js / PHP Next.js AEO / SEO / GEO GSC & GA4

### GitLite – Secure File Manager

**Problem:** Teams needed a simple, private place to store and share files.
**What was built:** A file manager on FastAPI and PostgreSQL that runs in containers (packaged software that is easy to move between servers).
**Result:** Each customer's files stay separate and secure, and the system can be moved to a new server without fuss.

FastAPI PostgreSQL Docker DevOps

**Problem:** Copying leads between spreadsheets, email tools and the CRM (customer list) wastes time and causes mistakes.
