# Automation As A Service and MSME Monetization

**Purpose:** Economic strategy for packaging industrial automation, robotics, and IIoT into recurring Opex-based "Automation-as-a-Service" (AaaS) models for small and medium manufacturing enterprises.

**Summary:** MSME factory owners often balk at massive ₹10L–₹50L upfront Capex for robotic cells and SCADA deployments. Shifting to an AaaS subscription (e.g. ₹35k–₹75k/month) unlocks massive enterprise adoption and creates predictable ARR.

---

## 1. Capex vs Opex (AaaS) Model Comparison

| Dimension | Legacy System Integrator (Capex) | Automation-as-a-Service (Opex) |
|---|---|---|
| **Upfront Barrier** | ₹10 Lakhs – ₹50 Lakhs upfront | Minimal setup / ₹50,000 security deposit |
| **Sales Cycle** | 6–18 months of budget negotiations | 2–4 weeks (treated as operational expense) |
| **Maintenance** | Factory left on its own after 1-yr warranty | Continuous cloud monitoring, SLA uptime guarantee |
| **Revenue Nature** | One-off, lump sum, seasonal | Predictable, sticky Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) |
| **Valuation Multiple**| Low (1–2x EBITDA for SI service) | High (6–12x ARR for software/AaaS platform) |
