# Ownership Beats Income

**Purpose:** To explain the structural advantage of owning equity over earning a salary.

**Summary:** High earners who stay employees rarely reach the top wealth tiers. True wealth is built by owning equity (a business, real estate, company stock) because ownership captures compounding value.

## Content
The single clearest pattern in wealth research (e.g., Federal Reserve Survey of Consumer Finances) is that high income does not equate to high wealth without ownership. 

A salary is structurally capped by the number of hours you can work and the market rate for your skills. Ownership (equity in a business, real estate, or stocks) separates your earnings from your time, allowing value to compound independently of your direct labor.

This is the actual mechanism of wealth creation, not a "mindset trick." People who get genuinely wealthy overwhelmingly own equity.
