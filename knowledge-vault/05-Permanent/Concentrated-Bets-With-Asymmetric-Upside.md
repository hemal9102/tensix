# Concentrated Bets With Asymmetric Upside

**Purpose:** To explain how early, concentrated investments drive outsized wealth, while acknowledging the survivorship bias.

**Summary:** Many wealthy individuals built their initial fortune through a few large, concentrated bets (startups, real estate). However, this strategy is highly risky and subject to massive survivorship bias.

## Content
Founders, early employees at equity-heavy startups, and early real estate investors in growing areas typically achieve wealth from a few large, concentrated bets, rather than diversified, small investments. This provides asymmetric upside (the potential gain far outweighs the potential loss).

However, it is crucial to recognize **survivorship bias**. For every highly visible success with this strategy, there are many unseen failures. While concentration builds wealth, diversification preserves it.
