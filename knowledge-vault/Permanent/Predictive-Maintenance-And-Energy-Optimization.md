# Predictive Maintenance and Energy Optimization

**Purpose:** Deconstruct algorithmic predictive maintenance (PdM) and factory energy optimization models, highlighting the shared-savings business model.

**Summary:** Industrial machines exhibit distinct physical degradation signatures (vibration, heat, acoustic emission, current draw) weeks before mechanical failure. Catching these early saves factories tens of lakhs in unplanned downtime, enabling highly profitable shared-savings service agreements.

---

## 1. Machine Degradation Signature Progression

```text
[Healthy State]
       │
       ▼
[Ultrasonic / Acoustic Anomaly]  ──► 3 to 6 months before failure
       │
       ▼
[Vibration FFT Signature Shift]  ──► 1 to 2 months before failure (Bearing / Gear defect)
       │
       ▼
[Thermal / Infrared Hotspot]     ──► 1 to 3 weeks before failure
       │
       ▼
[Audible Noise & Smoke]          ──► Hours before failure
       │
       ▼
[CATASTROPHIC MOTOR SEIZURE]     ──► ₹5L–₹25L Plant Stoppage Loss
```
