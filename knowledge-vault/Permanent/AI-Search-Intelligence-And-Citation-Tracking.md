# AI Search Intelligence and Citation Tracking

**Purpose:** Blueprint for building next-generation Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), and LLM citation monitoring platforms.

**Summary:** The internet search paradigm has fractured from 10 blue links into generative AI answers (Google AI Mode, ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Claude). The optimization problem shifts from "Rank #1 on keywords" to "Become the definitive, trusted entity that LLMs cite and recommend."

---

## 1. Traditional SEO vs Generative AI Search

| Feature | Legacy SEO | AI Search / AEO / GEO |
|---|---|---|
| **Discovery Mechanism** | Keyword indexing & backlinks | Entity graphs, vector embeddings, multi-step query fan-out |
| **Output** | SERP 10 blue links | Synthesized multi-source conversational answers |
| **User Objective** | Click through to website | Direct answer resolution or agentic action execution |
| **Optimization Vector** | Keyword density & page speed | Entity authority, Wikidata grounding, schema correctness |
