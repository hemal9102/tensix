# Time Horizon Over Timing

**Purpose:** To clarify the role of time compounding in investing versus the myth of market timing.

**Summary:** Long-term investing behavior studies show the biggest wealth differentiator isn't picking winning stocks, but staying invested for decades and not selling during downturns.

## Content
Long-term studies of investing behavior and behavioral finance consistently find that the primary driver of wealth from public markets is time compounding, not timing skill or "picking winners."

The biggest mistake investors make is selling during market downturns, interrupting the compounding process. Therefore, a long time horizon and the emotional discipline to stay invested are more critical to building wealth than attempting to time the market or find the perfect investment.
