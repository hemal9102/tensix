# Enterprise Engineering Case Studies

**Purpose:** To track how top technology companies deploy MAS and self-healing infrastructure at scale.

**Summary:** Real-time data and architecture blueprints for massive-scale MAS deployments are found in the engineering blogs of major tech companies. 

## Key Sources
1. **Google DeepMind & OpenAI Research:** For understanding the theoretical limits and upcoming capabilities of foundational models.
2. **Netflix Technology Blog:** Frequently publishes deep dives into self-healing infrastructure, chaos engineering, and how automated pipelines detect and resolve server failures without human intervention.
3. **Uber Engineering:** Provides blueprints for massive-scale data migrations, real-time dispatch optimizations, and distributed system architectures.

Studying these blogs provides the blueprint for building things like a self-healing PHP/Node.js pipeline that patches its own 500 errors in production.
