# Wealth Mindset Myths

**Purpose:** To debunk common internet myths surrounding wealth creation, such as the "millionaire mindset" and "7 income streams."

**Summary:** Much of the online content about getting rich confuses correlation with causation, relies heavily on survivorship bias, or is designed primarily to sell a course.

## Content
Several pervasive myths dominate the "make money online" space:

1. **Multiple Income Streams:** It is true that many wealthy people have several income sources. However, causally, it's usually reversed: the wealth from their *first* big win (a concentrated bet or business) allowed them the capital to build several streams. Trying to build 7 streams from scratch splits focus and leads to failure.
2. **The "Millionaire Mindset":** Content implying wealth is purely psychological ignores structural factors. While confidence and risk tolerance correlate with wealth, **survivorship bias** hides the many people who had the exact same mindset but lost their savings.
3. **Secret Systems:** If a course or guru is selling "the system rich people use," ask yourself: if it reliably made people rich, why does the seller need to sell the course? 
4. **Unverifiable Stats:** Claims like "what the top 1% do" are often invented for persuasive effect, not measured data.

The realistic path to monetizing skills (like research, coding, or scraping) is to pick a specific, underserved niche, build a genuinely useful tool or information asset over a year or more, and treat it like building equity.
