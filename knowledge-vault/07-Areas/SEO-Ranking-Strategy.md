# SEO Ranking Strategy

**Purpose:** The permanent answer to "why don't we rank in Ahmedabad, and what actually fixes it?"

**Summary:** On-page/AEO is done and competitive. The bottleneck is off-page authority. More schema/keywords/pages will NOT rank the head term — and mass thin programmatic pages can backfire on a DA-7 site.

## Content
**Diagnosis (confirmed from DA report + 5 competitor pages):** on-page SEO/AEO ≥ every competitor analyzed. Ranking gap = 100% off-page authority: DA 7, near-zero backlinks, 0 Google reviews.

**What works:**
1. Google Business Profile + aggressive review collection (Map Pack bypasses DA). Highest ROI.
2. Long-tail programmatic pages backed by *real* job data (locality/industry templates now render live listings + noindex-when-empty).
3. Local citations/backlinks: Sulekha, JustDial, ThreeBestRated, PlacementIndia, LinkedIn.
4. Authority compounds over 12–24 months.

**Anti-patterns (rejected):** spaced/unspaced keyword & hashtag injection (spam); government / Gujarat-Gas doorway pages we can't serve (Helpful-Content penalty risk); fake "Reviewed by expert" EEAT bylines (deceptive).

**Answer to "will it rank in Ahmedabad?":** Not the head term via on-page. Yes for long-tail + Map Pack.

## Lessons
- Stop re-deriving this. On-page is not the lever; authority is.
