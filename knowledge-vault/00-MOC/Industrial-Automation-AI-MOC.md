# Industrial Automation, AI & Physical Economy MOC

**Purpose:** Master Map of Content (MOC) cataloging the convergence of software engineering, AI agents, Industrial IoT, and Operational Technology (OT) across brownfield manufacturing, PLC/SCADA control loops, and physical infrastructure.

**Summary:** While most software developers compete in overcrowded web/app markets, massive enterprise value and high-moat opportunities exist where software meets messy, expensive physical industries. This graph explores Industrial AI, OT Cybersecurity, Automation-as-a-Service (AaaS), Machine Vision, and protocol engineering.
