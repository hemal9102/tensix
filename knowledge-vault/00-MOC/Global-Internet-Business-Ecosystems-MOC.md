# Global Internet Business Ecosystems & Opportunity Matrix (2026–2030) MOC

**Purpose:** Master Map of Content (MOC) taxonomizing the global online business landscape, ranking 12 macro ecosystems and 70+ venture categories by defensibility, asset compounding, and long-term tech moat.

**Summary:** The internet economy is transitioning from human-facing static websites toward a machine-first internet where AI agents, APIs, data graphs, and automated transaction networks intermediate demand and execution. This graph maps the transition from cash-flow ladders (Freelance/Agency) to compounding infrastructure (Agent OS, UCP Commerce, Financial Decision Engines, Proprietary Data).
