# Autonomous Software Economy & Self-Healing Agent Architecture MOC

**Purpose:** Master Map of Content (MOC) synthesizing the emergence of the Autonomous Software Economy, hierarchical multi-agent business operations (Agent CEO/CTO/CFO), controlled self-improving loops, Agent CI/CD (AgentOps), tool security, and autonomous economic capital management.

**Summary:** AI is transitioning from human-supervised assistance to autonomous software organizations capable of building, operating, supervising, repairing, and transacting through software. This graph models the architectural, security, and financial infrastructure necessary to deploy safe, self-healing, and deployable digital workers.
