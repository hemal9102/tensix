There are **hundreds of crawlers**, but you **should not list every bot in `robots.txt`**. A large robots.txt is harder to maintain and offers no ranking benefit. Only list bots when you want to give them different crawling rules.

For a modern SEO + AEO website like **TechAndCarsInfo** or **JobRecruitment.in**, here are the major bots grouped by purpose.

# Recommended robots.txt strategy

For most websites, don't create dozens of `User-agent` sections. Instead:

* Allow all legitimate search engines:

  ```text
  User-agent: *
  Allow: /
  ```

* Disallow only private areas:

  ```text
  Disallow: /admin/
  Disallow: /api/private/
  Disallow: /config/
  Disallow: /vendor/
  ```

* If you want to restrict AI training while remaining searchable, create **specific rules** for AI bots rather than blocking everything.

* Add:

  ```text
  Sitemap: https://example.com/sitemap.xml
  ```
