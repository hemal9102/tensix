# lead-generation-pipeline

**Location**: `skills/lead-generation-master-skill/SKILL.md`

## Description
Builds compliant B2B lead lists, conducts technical prospect audits, and generates cold outreach drafts. Use when finding B2B prospects, auditing business web listings, or drafting cold email campaigns. Do NOT use for general consumer (B2C) data, general SEO audits, or sending automated emails.
