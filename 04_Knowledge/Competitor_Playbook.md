# Rajput Bhavin's SEO/GEO Playbook

## 1. Technology Stack: Next.js & Zero JS Bloat
- **Next.js:** Leveraging Server-Side Rendering (SSR) and Static Site Generation (SSG) for ultra-fast page loads and immediate crawler indexing.
- **Zero JS Bloat:** Stripping away unnecessary JavaScript to minimize the Time to Interactive (TTI) and Largest Contentful Paint (LCP). Keeping the DOM light and relying on CSS for interactivity where possible.

## 2. Content Strategy: Dense, Short-Form Content
- **Length:** Hyper-focused, short blogs averaging around 250 words.
- **Density & Formatting:** High information density without fluff. Extensive use of `<strong>` tags to emphasize key terms, entities, and LSI (Latent Semantic Indexing) keywords, signaling importance directly to search engines.

## 3. Entity & Keyword Optimization
- **Technical LSI Entities:** Deep integration of Latent Semantic Indexing entities relevant to the topic. Moving beyond just keyword matching to building a knowledge graph of related concepts within the text.

## 4. Advanced Structured Data (JSON-LD)
Implementation of deep, complex JSON-LD structures to dominate SERP features and rich snippets:
- **`AggregateRating`:** Capturing review stars in search results to improve CTR.
- **`hasOfferCatalog`:** Structuring product or service offerings clearly for Google's shopping and product graphs.
- **`FAQPage`:** Providing direct Q&A structured data to capture "People Also Ask" boxes and voice search queries.
