# Psychological SEO Schemas

To win in both traditional SEO and Gen-AI (GEO/AEO) contexts, we apply deep psychological framing to our content. 

## 1. Event Schema
- **Trigger:** Immediate need, urgent hiring crisis, or hyper-growth phase.
- **Application:** Keywords targeting "urgent", "immediate", "crisis".
- **Content Framing:** Position our solution as the rapid-response mechanism. 
- **Links:** [[240_Keyword_Strategy#1 High-Intent Transactional]]

## 2. Social Schema
- **Trigger:** Peer validation, social proof, employer branding.
- **Application:** AEO queries about "best", "top-rated", "trusted".
- **Content Framing:** Use testimonials, case studies, and authority signals to dominate Answer Engine summaries.
- **Links:** [[240_Keyword_Strategy#2 Informational AEO Driven]]

## 3. Maladaptive Schema
- **Trigger:** Frustration with current providers, unmet expectations, feeling undervalued.
- **Application:** Targeting competitor weaknesses, especially in the [[Randstad_India_Battle_Plan]].
- **Content Framing:** Empathize with the pain of slow, impersonal, or mismatched recruitment from large agencies. Offer a boutique, high-precision alternative.
