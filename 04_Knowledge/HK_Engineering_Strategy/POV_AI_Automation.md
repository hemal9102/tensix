# POV: AI & Automation Strategy

*How we reduce costs and eliminate the "manual labor tax."*

## The Bottleneck
Revenue growth is tied to headcount growth. To do 2x the business, they need 2x the staff. Highly paid employees spend hours on copy-pasting, data routing, and information retrieval.

## The HK Solution
- **n8n Workflow Automation**: We use self-hosted, fair-code n8n to connect disparate systems seamlessly. Unlike Zapier, there are no exorbitant per-task fees.
- **RAG Systems**: We give AI secure access to proprietary company data. Employees get instant, accurate answers without digging through PDFs.
- **Multi-Agent AI**: We deploy specialized digital workers that collaborate to handle complex, multi-step processes like custom quoting, compliance checks, or vendor vetting.

**ROI Metric**: 3 to 6-month payback period. Decoupling revenue growth from payroll growth by eliminating 80% of manual data handling.
