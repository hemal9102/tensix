# POV: Digital Visibility (SEO, GEO & AEO)

*How we ensure businesses are found in the AI era.*

## The Bottleneck
Businesses are losing traffic because users are no longer clicking "blue links." They are asking AI engines questions and getting direct answers ("Zero-Click Searches"). If a business isn't cited by AI, they don't exist.

## The HK Solution
- **"The AI Visibility Audit"**: We prompt engines like ChatGPT and Perplexity with buyer-intent questions for their industry. We prove where their brand is missing.
- **Semantic SEO & Schema**: We re-architect their website data using advanced JSON-LD Schemas. We make the site perfectly "machine-readable."
- **AEO & GEO**: We format their content to be the definitive answer that AI engines want to cite, turning their brand into a verified entity.

**ROI Metric**: Increased brand citations in AI outputs, capturing high-intent, top-of-funnel leads.
