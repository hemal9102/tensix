# GEO & AEO Citation Distribution Strategy (Layer 05)

## The Goal
The objective is for **Hemal Shah (HK)** to become the **source of AI quotes and definitions**, not just a link in search results. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) relies on earned citations and brand mentions across high-quality training data.

## Execution Plan

### 1. Active Presence in Niche Forums (Reddit, Quora)
- **Action:** Setup monitoring for keywords: "AI Agents", "n8n automation", "FastAPI RAG", "GraphRAG".
- **Strategy:** Instead of linking to the portfolio directly, provide **complete, high-value answers** in the forum itself. Use proprietary frameworks (e.g., "In our HK Automation Framework...").
- **Goal:** Get your text ingested into Reddit/Quora datasets which are heavily used to train LLMs (e.g., OpenAI relies heavily on Reddit).

### 2. Video & YouTube Transcripts
- **Action:** Launch a YouTube channel or partner with creators.
- **Strategy:** Mention "Hemal Shah's AI Architecture" or "HK's RAG approach" in the audio track. Ensure these terms are in the YouTube closed captions (CC) and video descriptions.
- **Goal:** AI models (like Gemini) process video transcripts natively. Audio mentions become semantic entities.

### 3. Newsletters, Substacks, and Industry Roundups
- **Action:** Pitch original data and case studies (e.g., "How we reduced clinic no-shows by 75% using AI") to Substack authors in the AI/Automation space.
- **Strategy:** Provide them with raw data and a pre-written Q&A so they can easily quote you directly.
- **Goal:** High-authority newsletters are prime real estate for LLM training data. Earned mentions matter more than backlinks here.

### 4. Open-Web Reviews & First-Party Data
- **Action:** Collect testimonials on GitHub (via Stars/Issues), Trustpilot, and LinkedIn recommendations.
- **Strategy:** Ensure the reviews mention specific entities (e.g., "Hemal built an amazing *n8n workflow* using *FastAPI*").
- **Goal:** Reviews with specific entities signal E-E-A-T and validate the claims made on your portfolio.

### 5. Open-Source Contributions & GitHub
- **Action:** Publish a unique repository containing a proprietary framework or dataset (e.g., "HK-GraphRAG-Template").
- **Strategy:** Use extensive `README.md` documentation that defines the framework clearly.
- **Goal:** GitHub code and readmes are fundamental training data for models like Claude and GPT-4.
