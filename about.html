About TENSIX

An independent software and AI studio in Navrangpura, Ahmedabad. You work directly with Hemal Shah, the person who builds your project.

What TENSIX Does

TENSIX builds software that saves businesses time and money. It is based in Navrangpura, Ahmedabad and works with clients across India and abroad.

TENSIX is an agile engineering studio directed by Principal Systems Architect Hemal Shah, a software and AI engineer. There is no sales team and no account manager in between. The person you talk to is the person writing your code. Hemal uses AI tools to handle routine work, so projects move faster without cutting corners.

What you can hire TENSIX for:

  • AI assistants and agents that answer questions from your own documents or handle repetitive tasks.
  • Custom software and SaaS apps (online products your customers log in to and use).
  • Fast, mobile-friendly websites that are easy to find on Google.
  • Server setup and hosting, with automatic updates and monitoring so problems are caught early.
  • Data collection and automation that pulls information from websites or tools into one place.
  • Search visibility on Google and AI answer tools like ChatGPT and Perplexity.

Past projects include a doctor appointment booking platform, tools that collect business data from the web, and an AI system that helps put together textbook content. Every project is built to be simple to run, easy to hand over, and fully owned by the client.

Common Questions

Straight answers on how TENSIX works, what it costs, and who you will be dealing with.

How is TENSIX different from a typical IT company?

Most IT companies put managers and junior developers between you and the work, and bill by the hour. TENSIX is an independent studio founded by Hemal Shah, in Navrangpura, Ahmedabad. You speak directly with the person building your project. Work is priced per project or per month, agreed in writing before it starts, and Hemal uses AI tools to finish routine tasks faster.

Can you sign an NDA or work white-label for my agency?

Yes. TENSIX can sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) before you share any details. Agencies in India and abroad can also hire TENSIX white-label, which means the work is delivered under your brand. You own all the code from day one, and it is handed over in your own GitHub account.

Can I hire TENSIX on a monthly basis?

Yes. Dedicated studio retainers start at ₹79,999 ($999) a month. The Autonomous Systems Pod is ₹1,49,999 ($1,850) a month, and our Fractional CTO & Enterprise Architecture engagement is ₹2,49,999 ($3,200) a month. Hemal works on your project every month, shares progress in your GitHub account, and you can message him directly.

Have a project in mind?

Tell us what you want to fix or build. You will get a plain answer on whether it makes sense, how long it takes and what it costs.
