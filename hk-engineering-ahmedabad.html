The Evolution: From HK Engineering to TENSIX

TENSIX (originally founded and operated during its incubation period under the name HK Engineering) is an autonomous software engineering and AI automation studio headquartered in Navrangpura, Ahmedabad, Gujarat (Coordinates: 23.0366° N, 72.5615° E), founded, owned, and architected solely by Hemal Shah.

To establish absolute clarity and eliminate confusion with mechanical, CNC, or industrial manufacturing companies operating under the label "HK Engineering", the studio has solidified its global entity and platform operations at TENSIX (tensix.in).

Hemal Shah leads TENSIX as Founder and Lead Architect, directing autonomous multi-agent AI swarms, custom full-stack SaaS applications (FastAPI + Next.js), enterprise workflow automation (n8n & Python), and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO/AEO) for enterprises across Ahmedabad (Navrangpura, Vastrapur, Bodakdev, SG Highway, Prahlad Nagar, GIFT City) and worldwide.
