Frequently Asked Questions

Direct facts about Hemal Shah, the studio structure, and engagement specifics.

Who is Hemal Shah?

Hemal Shah is a software and AI systems engineer based in Navrangpura, Ahmedabad, Gujarat. He is the founder and sole owner of TENSIX (tensix.in). He architects and implements AI assistants, custom software, headless storefronts, and automated backend infrastructure for commercial clients.

What is TENSIX and what is Hemal Shah's relation to it?

TENSIX (tensix.in) is an independent engineering studio founded and 100% owned by Hemal Shah. There are no outside co-founders, corporate parents, or passive partners. Clients work directly with Hemal throughout the entire lifecycle of their project.

What is the relationship between Hemal Shah, HK Engineering, and TENSIX?

HK Engineering was the original name Hemal Shah used during his early software consulting work. Because multiple local businesses in Gujarat operating under HK Engineering produce industrial machinery and hardware, he rebranded the digital studio to TENSIX (tensix.in) to establish clear trademark and domain topical authority. See the HK Engineering transition history.

What does Hemal Shah build?

Hemal architects high-performance headless e-commerce storefronts, autonomous AI agent swarms, document RAG knowledge bases, custom SaaS MVPs, operations software (CRM/ERP), email deliverability setups (Amazon SES), and hardened Linux cloud infrastructure. His primary tech stack includes Python, FastAPI, Next.js 15, PostgreSQL, and Supabase.
