When TENSIX Fits, and When It Does Not

A one-person studio is not the right choice for every project.

A good fit

  • Focused projects: an AI assistant, an automation, a web app, a website or a cloud setup.
  • You want to deal directly with the engineer and see progress every week.
  • You want a fixed price and full ownership of what is built.

Another option may suit you better

  • You need a large team working on many things at once.
  • You need staff available around the clock to answer support calls.
  • You mainly need branding, advertising or social media campaigns.

Two Common Choices Explained Simply

Ready-made tools are often the right start. Here is when a custom build makes more sense.

A Custom AI Assistant vs a Ready-Made Tool (e.g. ChatGPT Team)

Custom AI assistant (built by TENSIX)

  • Works with your own systems: it can look up orders, customers or stock in your database or CRM and take action.
  • Runs on your server: your business data stays under your control.
  • No per-user fee: you pay for the AI usage itself, not a monthly seat for every staff member. See AI agent development.

Ready-made tool (ChatGPT Team / Enterprise)

  • Quick to start: great for writing, research and general questions with no setup.
  • Priced per user per month, so the cost grows as your team grows.
  • Limited links to your systems: connecting it to your own software or old ERP can be hard.

Self-Hosted n8n vs Zapier for Business Automation

Self-hosted n8n (set up by TENSIX)

  • Flat cost: runs on your own server, so you pay the server bill, not a fee per task.
  • Handles complex steps: custom code can be added when a workflow needs it.
  • Needs looking after: someone must keep the server updated. TENSIX sets up monitoring and automatic restarts. See automation services.

Zapier / Make

  • Easy to start: thousands of ready connections and no server to manage.
  • Usage-based pricing: bills rise as the number of tasks grows.
  • Less flexible for unusual workflows, which can need workarounds.
