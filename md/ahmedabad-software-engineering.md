What are GEO and AEO, and how are they different from SEO? +

SEO helps you rank in normal Google results. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) and AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) help AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews understand your business and mention it in their answers. We do this with clear question-and-answer pages and structured data (labels that tell search engines who you are and what you offer). Nobody can guarantee placement, but this makes your business easier to find and quote.

What can an AI agent do for my business? +

An AI agent is software that can read, decide and act on simple tasks. For example, it can answer customer questions from your own documents, sort incoming emails, pull details from invoices or draft replies for you to approve. We build these with well-known AI models and add checks so answers stay based on your own information.

Why do my emails land in spam, and can you fix it? +

Emails often land in spam because the domain is missing the records that prove the mail really comes from you: SPF, DKIM and DMARC. We set these up correctly, connect a reliable sending service such as Amazon SES, and handle bounced emails properly. This greatly improves inbox delivery, though no one can promise every email reaches the inbox.
