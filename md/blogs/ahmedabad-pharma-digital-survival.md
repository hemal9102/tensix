# Why Ahmedabad Pharma Firms Lose Export Buyers, and How to Stop It

August 20265 min read

**In plain words**

This article is about how a weak or hacked website can cost Ahmedabad pharma companies their international buyers. It is for owners and export managers of pharma manufacturers, especially if your website was built years ago and is rarely updated. Foreign buyers check you online before they ever send an inquiry. The one takeaway: keep your website secure and up to date, and connect product information and inquiries to your internal systems so buyers get fast, correct answers.

Ahmedabad is a major pharma manufacturing hub. Yet many established firms are losing international and Indian buyers. The problem is usually not product quality. It is a website and online presence that does not inspire confidence.

## Old WordPress Sites Are an Easy Target

Many pharma companies still run old WordPress websites that are rarely updated. A well-known attack called the "Pharma Hack" targets exactly these sites. It hides spam code that sends visitors to fake medicine pages. Google may then show a warning or drop the site from its results. When a foreign buyer sees that, years of reputation can disappear quickly.

## Buyers Research You Before They Call

B2B buyers research suppliers online, and more of them now ask AI assistants too. If your product details, certifications and contact options are hard to find, they move on. When inquiries are handled by hand, replies are slow and details get missed. Connecting your website to your ERP (the software you use to run stock and orders) helps buyers get quick, accurate answers.
