## 3. Core Capabilities

### 3.1 Sub-400ms Global Edge Routing
Zero-bloat HTML compiled and deployed across global edge networks (Cloudflare / Vercel). When an international procurement officer in Frankfurt, Chicago, or Dubai requests specifications, TTFB is under 400ms without origin server lag.

### 3.2 Technical Spec & CAD Data Mesh
Transforms engineering drawings, dimensional diagrams, and PDF product catalogs into searchable, structured data. Buyers can filter machinery by operating pressure, material grades, tolerance specs, and power ratings right on the page.

### 3.3 Connected `@graph` Entity Architecture
Full JSON-LD graph connecting the manufacturer entity with its ISO certifications, plant facilities, Wikidata IDs, and founder credentials, enabling zero-shot citation by Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, and Google AI Overviews.

### 3.4 Automated RFQ to ERP Pipelines
Captures custom engineering tolerances, volumetric order requirements, and CAD drawings into validated JSON payloads. Routes inquiries directly into SAP, Tally Prime, Zoho CRM, and company WhatsApp dispatch channels in under 500ms.
