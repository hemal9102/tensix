Nothing else changed. Hemal still owns and runs the studio on his own, and still works with businesses across Ahmedabad (Navrangpura, Vastrapur, Bodakdev, SG Highway, Prahlad Nagar, GIFT City) and abroad. You always talk directly to the person doing the work.
